My Process

From idea to interface.

A structured but flexible workflow I follow on every project, from first spark to final handoff.

01 Discover

Discover

Understand the core idea, audience, real friction points, and overarching project goals.

02 Research

Research

Explore user expectations, audit competitor patterns, gather visual moodboards and benchmark systems.

03 Define

Define

Synthesize key requirements, map user flows, and lock in the architecture and content hierarchy.

04 Design

Design

Draft low-fi wireframes, scale into design tokens, render UI screens, and link interactive prototypes.

05 Refine

Refine

Conduct usability walkthroughs, polish micro-details, prepare design specs, and inspect developer handoff.
